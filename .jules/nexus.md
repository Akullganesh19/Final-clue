## 2025-07-04 — Serial Pattern Clustering
**Product understood as:** A multi-agent case linkage system designed to find connections between individual cold cases based on semantic, entity, temporal, and MO signals.
**Derivation reasoning:** We store `Case` data and compute pairwise `Linkage` records. Users can review these pairwise connections to find patterns. Therefore, users obviously need the ability to see macro-patterns formed by multiple connected cases — because serial offenders rarely stop at two crimes, and finding interconnected webs of cases is the ultimate goal of cold-case triage. It doesn't exist because the initial implementation stopped at the pairwise correlation phase (the `Linkage` entity). I'm building it because giving investigators a high-level view of multi-case clusters will drastically reduce triage time and highlight the most active/dangerous serial networks.
**Feature built:** Added `CaseCluster` type to core types and implemented `buildCaseClusters` using a graph traversal (DFS) algorithm to group pairwise linkages into macro pattern clusters. Tested for complex discrete groupings and confidence filtering.
**User impact:** Investigators will be able to look beyond one-to-one matches and visualize complete suspected serial networks, prioritizing investigations based on cluster size and average linkage confidence.
**Next logical feature:** Generating automated generative AI summaries of what the entire `CaseCluster` has in common to establish a unified suspect profile.
