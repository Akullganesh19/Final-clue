## 2025-03-05 — Next-Action Prediction Engine

**Product understood as:** A multi-agent case-linkage & evidence-triage system for cold cases. It creates a heavy audit trail for all investigator operations.

**Prediction invented:** A Next-Action Prediction engine (`ActionPredictor`) that tracks and builds a Markov chain out of an investigator's `AuditTrail` history. It predicts the most likely next action and automatically triggers a low-priority, credentialed background prefetch for the associated data (e.g. predicting a user will `VIEW_EVIDENCE_LIST` after they `VIEW_CASE_LIST`).

**Data used:** The existing `AuditTrail` generated by `createAuditLog` in `src/utils/audit.ts`. Signal is the sequential nature of `action` strings stored in this log.

**Impact:** Investigators experience near-instant load times for pages they predictably visit next. The app feels like it knows what they are looking for before they click.

**Next opportunity:** Predicting intelligent default filter sets based on historically frequent search queries.
